# UnSent

> *These were not meant to be read. Read them anyway.*

A personal archive by **Om Pranab Mohanty** — a quiet corner of the internet where unfinished thoughts, unpolished words, and unsent feelings find a place to exist.

---

## What is this?

**UnSent** is a personal writing archive. Not a blog. Not a portfolio. Just a place where words that refused to stay quiet finally landed.

The writing here is raw by design — unfiltered, unrevised, unashamed. These are the pieces that arrived without warning and left before they could be shaped into something neat. They were never meant to be finished. They were never meant to be read. And yet, here they are.

---

## What lives here

The archive holds two kinds of writing:

- **Prose** — fragments, reflections, and thoughts that didn't quite know what they wanted to be
- **Poems** — words arranged the way they felt, not the way they were supposed to

There's no schedule. No theme. No agenda. Just necessity.

---

## The spirit of it

> *I do not write by habit, only by necessity.*

Everything here arrived uninvited. Nothing here was planned. If something resonates, it was probably written at 2am and never looked at again. That's the point.
