UnSent

Om Pranab Mohanty

These were not
meant to be read.
Read them anyway.

I do not write by habit, only by necessity. Most of what appears here arrives without warning and leaves before it can be shaped. These pieces were not meant to be finished, revised, or explained. What lives here is unfiltered, unpolished, and unashamed. These are the thoughts that refused to stay quiet.
